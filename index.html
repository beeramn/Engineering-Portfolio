About Me

I'm Brandon Ramirez Argueta, an Electrical & Computer Engineering and Computer Science student at Duke University with a strong interest in digital hardware, embedded systems, and computer architecture. I enjoy building systems where hardware and software come together. My projects have ranged from designing a custom 32-bit pipelined processor in Verilog and deploying it on an FPGA, to developing custom PCBs and embedded control systems, to building machine-learning and full-stack software applications. Through these experiences, I've worked across FPGA development, digital logic, PCB design, embedded programming, machine learning, and software engineering. I'm especially interested in continuing to deepen my experience in digital design and hardware development while working toward a career building high-performance computing and semiconductor systems.
